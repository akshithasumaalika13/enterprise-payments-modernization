# Data Governance Framework

## Example glossary term
**Net Payment Value**: Gross settled payment value less refunds and chargebacks.

## Ownership
- Finance marts: Finance Analytics
- Product marts: Product Analytics
- Platform pipelines: Data Engineering

## Classification
- PII: email, customer name, device identifiers
- Confidential: transaction amounts, balances
- Restricted: full account numbers, secrets, tokens

## Controls
- Least privilege
- Row/column masking
- Data-owner assignment
- Lineage and certification
- SLA/freshness tracking
