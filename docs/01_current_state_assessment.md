# Current-State Assessment

## Client
NorthStar Financial Services — fictional portfolio client.

## Current-state issues
1. Finance and operations totals are reconciled manually.
2. Payment failure visibility is delayed.
3. KPI definitions differ across business units.
4. Product teams cannot consistently measure funnels, retention, or experiment outcomes.
5. Pipeline ownership and lineage are unclear.
6. Sensitive data lacks standardized classification and access controls.
7. Deployment is manual and testing is inconsistent.

## Risks
- Incorrect financial reporting
- Revenue leakage
- Poor customer experience
- Slow incident response
- Audit/control weakness
- High operational cost
